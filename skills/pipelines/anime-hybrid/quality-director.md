# Anime Hybrid — Independent QA and Final Director

L0 checks contract, hashes, media, subtitle/safe-area, audio, and license. Identity/continuity review is a separate timecoded L1 check and cannot turn an L0 failure into PASS.

After compose, run `temporal_analysis` then `limited_grammar` before `visual_review`. Sparse keyframes are not SEE. STATIC_HOLD is a measurement, not a fail. Critique without a temporal motion report is incomplete.

At the Final Gate record accept, bounded repair, or abandon. Packaging never auto-publishes and writes retention/provenance evidence.
